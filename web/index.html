Critical Analysis: “What is misleading about my headline number?”

1. Class Imbalance Frequency Skew

Standard support datasets are dominated by routine inquiries. A high headline accuracy can easily mask dangerous failure rates on rare existential hazards (e.g. thermal runaway / battery explosions).

2. Asymmetric Cost of Errors

Auto-handling an explosive battery or account fraud carries catastrophic liability ($1,000+), while false-escalating a battery health query costs only $5 of agent labor. Headline accuracy treats both errors as equal.

3. Evaluation Leakage Shortcuts

Random train/test splits allow memorization of canned customer support templates. Graph-level conversation partitioning is mandatory for authentic generalization.
